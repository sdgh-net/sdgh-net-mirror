课堂教学用语
顺德市国华纪念中学教学处
2002年9月1日

Classroom  English

1、上课                       Class   begins

2、今天谁值日?                 Who’s  on  duty  today?

3、人都到齐了吗?               Is everyone  here?

4、今天星期几?                 what day is today?

5、今天星期一（二、三、四、五、六、日）       It’s Monday.<Tuesday. Wednesday.Thursday. Friday. Saturday. Sunday.>

6、请打开书                   Open your books, please.

7、有问题吗?                  Do you have any question?

8、请保持安静                 Keep quiet. please！<Bequiet>                                                                                      

9、请举手                     Raise your hands, please.                                  

10、请到前面来                Please come to the front.

11、坐直了〈坐好〉            Sit up.

12、请拿出笔记本              Please take out your notebook.

13、我们做一下听写            Let’s have a dictation.

14、今天我们上新课            We’re going to have a new lesson today.

15、请听我讲                 Listen to me. Please.

16、跟我读                   Repeat after me.

17、准备好了吗               Are you ready?  Ready?

18、请看黑板（幻灯）          Look at the blackboard<slide>, please.

19、不要望窗外               Don’t look out of the window.

20、请回答我的问题            Answer my question, please.

21、不要讲小话               Don’t mumble.

22、别紧张                  Don’t be nervous.

23、我们一起齐声朗读，预备，起。 Let’s read it together<in chorus>. Ready go.

24、请读慢一点 ，清楚一点。    Read slowly and clearly, please.

25、完成了吗?                Are you through?

26、做得不错。               You did a good job.

27、不错。                   Terrific！

28、请给他（她）一些掌声。     Give him<her> a hand.

29、时间到了，下课。          Time’s up. Class is over.

30、让我们玩个游戏            Let’s play a game.

31、喜欢这个游戏吗？          Did<do> you like the game ?

32、这个故事有意思，不是吗？   The story is very interesting, Isn’t it.

33、累了吗？                 Are you tired？

34、听音乐 ，放松一下。       Listen to the music and just relax.

35、休息一下。               Let’s take a break.

36、请注意                  All eyes on me<up there>

37、能听明白吗？             Can you follow me<keep up with me>?

38、听明白了吗？            Did you follow me?

39、请同位同学讨论。         Discuss it with your partner, Please.                 

40、 谁（先）试试？          Who wants to try<go><first>?

41、很好的尝试。            Good try.

42、来吧，你就快对了。      Come on .you are  almost there.

43、来吧，你能说<做到>的    Come on .you can say<DO> It.

44、我给你一个暗示。         I  will give you a clue <hint>.

45、谁能帮帮他（她）？      Who can help him<her>?

46、我不想你纠正错误。    I don’t want you to correct the mistakes.

47、准备好了吗？              Are you ready?

48、你能猜猜吗?                Can you guess it? Take a guess.               

49、我有一些任务给你们做。     I have some work for you to do.

50、到谁了？                           Whose turn is it?

51、不要说，请举手。                   Just hands. No voice.

52、自己做。                            Do it on your own.

53、不要抄别人的。                      Without copying.

54、请关门。                      Don’t forget to close the door.

55、这是今天的家庭作业。今天没有家庭作业。 

Here is your homework for today. No  homework  today.

56、回答问题前，请举手。            Hands up before you answer.                      

57、家庭作业最迟星期五交。 Hand in homework Friday at the latest.

58、下课后来找我                  Come and see me after class.

59、下课后到我办公室来。          Come to my office after class.

60、 对，你是对的。                      Yes. You’re  right.

61、好吗，好的。                        Alright.?Alright.

62、好。不错。就这样。好样的，好多了。

Well done.Not bad. That is it. Good for you. Much better.

63、好主意，有道理。               Good idea. That makes sense.

64、不是。差不多是。          Not really. Almost right. Quiet near.

65、对不起。能再说一次吗？    I’m sorry. Can you say that again?

66、一个接一个。                   One by one.

67、慢慢来。                       Take your time.

68、齐声读（唱，说）                   Read<sing, say>in chorus.

69、 大声点。                        Loudly<louder>

70、 别说话。                        No talking.

71、 请仔细听。                     Please listen carefully.

72、请跟我读。                     Please repeat after me.

73、谁缺席？                       Who is absent?

74、今天我们就到这里吧？          Can we stop here for today?

75、谁能帮助他（她）？             Who can help him <her>?

76、我们休息一会儿。                Let’s have a break.

77、做作业时安静，不要影响别人。

      Work quietly. Don’t  disturb the others.

78、言行举止要文明。    Be kind with your words and your actions.

79、尊重别人。                           Respect others.

80、动脑筋想想。                         Use your head.
